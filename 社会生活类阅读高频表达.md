# 社会生活类阅读高频表达

> 精选60个考研英语社会类高频短语+句式，按场景分类

---

## 一、描述社会变迁

| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **demographic shift** | 人口结构变化 | *The demographic shift toward an older population has major implications for social services.* |
| **social fabric** | 社会结构 | *Volunteering helps to strengthen the social fabric.* |
| **reshape the landscape** | 重塑格局 | *Social media has reshaped the media landscape.* |
| **a far cry from** | 与…相差甚远 | *Today's job market is a far cry from that of a generation ago.* |
| **transform A into B** | 将A变为B | *The internet has transformed how we consume news.* |
| **gain traction** | 获得关注/势头 | *The movement for mental health awareness is gaining traction.* |
| **a paradigm shift** | 范式转变 | *Remote work represents a paradigm shift in workplace culture.* |

## 二、描述社会问题

| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **pose a threat to** | 对…构成威胁 | *Misinformation poses a serious threat to democratic discourse.* |
| **take a toll on** | 对…造成损害 | *Social media addiction has taken a toll on teenage mental health.* |
| **be plagued by** | 受…困扰 | *The healthcare system is plagued by chronic underfunding.* |
| **widen the gap** | 扩大差距 | *The digital divide widens the gap between urban and rural students.* |
| **exacerbate** | 加剧；恶化 | *Algorithmic recommendations exacerbate political polarization.* |
| **a double-edged sword** | 双刃剑 | *Social media is a double-edged sword for adolescent development.* |
| **a perfect storm** | 多重不利因素叠加 | *A perfect storm of rising tuition and stagnant wages has created a student debt crisis.* |

## 八、写作高分句型

### 开头
- *Few social issues have generated as much debate as [X].*
- *The question of [X] has taken on new urgency in recent years.*

### 转折（最经典）
- ***While [A], [B].** — 尽管A，但是B*
- *For all the benefits of [X], there are costs to weigh.*
- *The picture, however, is more complicated than it first appears.*

### 因果链
- *This, in turn, leads to [C].*
- *The net result is [D].*

### 总结
- *There are no easy answers; what is needed is a nuanced approach that balances [A] with [B].*
- *Ultimately, the challenge is not [X] but [Y].*
