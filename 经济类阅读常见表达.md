# 经济类阅读常见表达

> 精选 100 个考研英语经济类文章中的高频短语、固定搭配和地道表达
> 按使用场景分类，适合写作和翻译模仿

---

## 一、描述趋势（上升/下降/变化）

### 上升
| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| soar / skyrocket | 飙升 | *Inflation has soared to a 40-year high.* |
| surge | 激增 | *Oil prices surged after the supply disruption.* |
| spike | 急剧上升 | *Unemployment claims spiked in the first week of lockdowns.* |
| rally / rebound | 反弹/回升 | *Stocks rallied after the Fed signaled a pause in rate hikes.* |
| pick up | 加快/好转 | *Economic growth is expected to pick up in the second half.* |
| climb / edge up | 爬升/小幅上升 | *The jobless rate edged up to 3.8%.* |
| gain ground | 取得进展 | *The dollar gained ground against the euro.* |

### 下降
| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| plunge / plummet | 骤降 | *The stock plunged 30% in a single day.* |
| tumble | 暴跌 | *Home prices tumbled as foreclosures flooded the market.* |
| slump | 暴跌/不景气 | *Exports slumped as global demand weakened.* |
| slide / slip | 下滑 | *The currency slid to a five-year low.* |
| shrink / contract | 收缩 | *The economy contracted for two consecutive quarters.* |
| dip | 小幅下降 | *Retail sales dipped 0.3% in October.* |
| take a hit | 受打击 | *Consumer confidence took a hit from rising prices.* |

### 平稳/恢复
| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| stabilize / level off | 企稳 | *House prices appear to be leveling off after years of gains.* |
| bottom out | 触底 | *The housing market bottomed out in early 2024.* |
| recover / bounce back | 复苏/回弹 | *The economy bounced back faster than expected.* |
| hold steady | 保持稳定 | *The unemployment rate held steady at 3.7%.* |
| flatline | 持平 | *Wages have flatlined for the bottom half of earners.* |

## 二、描述因果关系

| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| fuel / drive | 推动/助长 | *Easy credit fueled the housing bubble.* |
| trigger / spark | 引发/触发 | *The collapse of Lehman Brothers triggered a global panic.* |
| give rise to | 引起 | *Rising rates give rise to concerns about debt sustainability.* |
| stem from | 源于 | *The crisis stemmed from reckless lending practices.* |
| be attributed to | 归因于 | *The slowdown is attributed to sluggish consumer spending.* |
| result in / lead to | 导致 | *The trade war resulted in higher prices for consumers.* |
| be driven by | 由…驱动 | *Growth was driven by exports and infrastructure spending.* |
| translate into | 转化为 | *Productivity gains have not translated into higher wages.* |
| ripple through | 波及 | *The shock rippled through global financial markets.* |
| spill over into | 溢出/波及 | *The housing slump spilled over into the broader economy.* |

## 三、描述对比与矛盾

| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| outpace / outstrip | 超过/胜过 | *Wage growth has failed to outpace inflation.* |
| lag behind | 落后 | *Europe lags behind the U.S. in AI investment.* |
| dwarf | 使相形见绌 | *China's infrastructure spending dwarfs that of any other nation.* |
| pale in comparison to | 相形见绌 | *The 2008 crisis pales in comparison to the Great Depression.* |
| on a par with | 与…相当 | *Its GDP per capita is now on a par with Portugal's.* |
| a far cry from | 相差甚远 | *Today's economy is a far cry from the booming 1990s.* |
| at odds with | 与…矛盾 | *The data is at odds with the central bank's rosy forecast.* |

## 四、描述影响与重要性

| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| weigh on | 对…构成压力 | *Rising rates weigh on corporate profits.* |
| take a toll on | 对…造成损害 | *Inflation has taken a toll on household budgets.* |
| squeeze | 挤压/收紧 | *Rising costs are squeezing profit margins.* |
| underpin | 支撑 | *Strong consumer spending underpins the recovery.* |

## 六、描述困境与风险

| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| be on the brink of | 濒临/处于…边缘 | *The country is on the brink of default.* |
| teeter on the edge of | 摇摇欲坠于 | *The economy is teetering on the edge of recession.* |
| be saddled with | 背负（沉重负担） | *Graduates are saddled with record levels of debt.* |
| be plagued by | 受…困扰 | *The sector is plagued by overcapacity.* |
| face headwinds | 面临阻力/逆风 | *The industry faces headwinds from rising raw material costs.* |
| a double-edged sword | 双刃剑 | *A strong currency is a double-edged sword for exporters.* |
| be in the doldrums | 处于低迷/不景气 | *The shipping industry has been in the doldrums.* |
| brace for | 为…做好准备（通常是不好的事） | *Markets are bracing for a prolonged trade war.* |

## 九、地道比喻表达（外刊高频）

| 表达 | 字面义 | 实际含义 |
|------|--------|----------|
| **safe haven** | 安全港湾 | 避险资产（如黄金、美债） |
| **gold rush** | 淘金热 | 逐利热潮（如 AI gold rush） |
| **perfect storm** | 完美风暴 | 多重不利因素同时发生 |
| **silver lining** | 乌云的金边 | 困境中的一线希望 |
| **safety net** | 安全网 | 社会保障制度 |
| **level playing field** | 平整的赛场 | 公平竞争环境 |
| **race to the bottom** | 逐底竞争 | （各国）竞相压低标准争抢 |
| **elephant in the room** | 房间里的大象 | 人人回避的明显问题 |
| **tip of the iceberg** | 冰山一角 | 只是表面，问题更深 |
| **kick the can down the road** | 把罐子踢远 | 拖延问题而不解决 |
| **cushion the blow** | 缓冲打击 | 减轻冲击 |
| **wage war on** | 向…宣战 | 大力打击（wage war on inflation） |
| **throw money at** | 往…砸钱 | 用钱解决但不一定有效 |
| **tighten one's belt** | 勒紧腰带 | 紧缩/省着花 |

## 十、写作/翻译加分句型

### 开头引出话题
- *The debate over [X] has intensified in recent years.*
- *Few economic issues have generated as much controversy as [X].*
- *[X] has emerged as a defining economic challenge of our time.*

### 表达观点
- *The evidence suggests that...*
- *A growing body of research indicates that...*
- *It is widely acknowledged that...*

### 转折与让步
- *While [A], [B].*（尽管A，但是B）— 最经典的考研句式
- *For all the benefits of [X], there are costs to weigh.*
- *Yet set against these benefits are a bundle of worries.*

### 因果链条
- *The logic is straightforward: if [A], then [B].*
- *This, in turn, leads to [C].*
- *The net result is [D].*

### 总结建议
- *Policymakers would be well-advised to...*
- *A more nuanced approach is needed — one that balances [A] with [B].*
- *There are no silver bullets; what is needed is a combination of [X] and [Y].*

## 记忆建议

1. **每次读外刊画短语**，不只画单词
2. **按场景背**：一天一类（如今天只背"趋势"类）
3. **仿写**：每学一个句型，用自己关心的经济话题写一句
4. **同义替换**：写作时有意替换基础词——用 surge 代替 increase，用 plunge 代替 decrease
5. **比喻要理解故事**："kick the can down the road"想象踢罐子的画面
