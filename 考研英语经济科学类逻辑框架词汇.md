# 考研英语经济科学类逻辑框架词汇

> **阅读理解的关键能力**：在科技 × 经济的交叉文章中，快速识别逻辑骨架和作者立场
> **适用范围**：考研英语（一/二）阅读 Part A 精读 + Part B 七选五
> **核心公式**：转折词后 = 作者真实态度

---

## 一、科技经济类文章的独特阅读难点

经济科学类文章（如"AI 对就业的影响""新能源补贴的争议"）有三大阅读难点：

| 难点 | 表现 | 对策 |
|------|------|------|
| **双重术语负担** | 既有经济术语（inflation, subsidy）又有科技术语（algorithm, genome） | 基础术语靠词汇笔记，逻辑框架词定位 |
| **观点快速切换** | 科技乐观派 vs 技术怀疑派的交替引用 | 盯紧转折词，谁被 but 了就是作者的靶子 |
| **事实与观点的混淆** | 作者可能用数据事实推进，但自己的价值判断在逻辑词后 | 因果词=事实逻辑链，转折词=价值判断 |

---

## 二、科技经济文章专属转折链

> 经济科学类文章最常见的套路：**先承认技术的好处 → but → 讨论问题/风险/副作用**

### 核心套路词

| 结构 | 信号词 | 阅读策略 |
|------|--------|---------|
| **承认+转折** | It is true that... but... / no doubt... however... / admittedly... yet... | 前面是靶子，后面是作者的真实观点 |
| **乐观+但** | Technology has the potential to... but... / This promises... nevertheless... | "潜力巨大"后面一定接"但也带来问题" |
| **数据+转折** | According to data... however... / The figures show... but... | 数据是铺垫，but 后的定性才是考点 |
| **专家观点+但** | Some researchers argue... whereas others... / Some warn that... while others... | 对比双方观点，注意作者站在哪一边 |

### 真题示例

> **原文：** "AI has **no doubt** improved productivity in manufacturing. **However**, its impact on service sectors has been far more disruptive than initially anticipated."

> **解读：** no doubt 承认好处 → However 引出真问题（服务业的颠覆性影响）。命题人必定在这里出题。

## 三、经济科学类高频因果链

科技经济文章中因果关系常跨越多个环节，需要梳理**因果链**而非单层因果。

### 典型因果链模式

```
技术发展 (cause)
  → 生产力提升 (immediate effect)
    → 就业结构变化 (secondary effect)
      → 收入不平等加剧 (tertiary effect)
        → 政策响应需求 (ultimate effect)
```

### 因果链连接词

| 链式因果词 | 含义 | 科技经济文章中的常见用法 |
|-----------|------|------------------------|
| **trigger a chain reaction** | 触发连锁反应 | *Automation triggered a chain reaction of job restructuring.* |
| **ripple effect** | 涟漪效应 | *The chip shortage had a ripple effect across the global economy.* |
| **cascade** | 级联 | *Tech layoffs cascaded from the top to the entire ecosystem.* |
| **feedback loop** | 反馈循环 | *The feedback loop between data collection and algorithm improvement drives platform dominance.* |
| **virtuous / vicious cycle** | 良性/恶性循环 | *Network effects create a virtuous cycle for market leaders.* |
| **amplify** | 放大 | *Social media amplifies economic inequality by concentrating attention revenue.* |
| **compound** | 使加重 | *The pandemic compounded existing technological disparities.* |

## 四、比较与对比：科技 × 经济的跨界对比

经济科学类文章频繁出现**跨界对比**——将科技概念与经济概念并列表述。

| 对比词 | 科技经济文章中常对比什么 |
|--------|------------------------|
| **unlike** | *Unlike previous industrial revolutions, AI affects both blue-collar and white-collar jobs.* |
| **whereas / while** | *While technology giants enjoyed record profits, workers in traditional sectors saw stagnant wages.* |
| **by contrast** | *China's EV industry is booming; by contrast, legacy automakers are struggling.* |
| **the same is true of** | *Network effects create monopolies; the same is true of data-driven markets.* |
| **pale in comparison to** | *The efficiency gains pale in comparison to the social costs of displacement.* |

### 阅读策略

```
看到科技 × 经济的跨界对比 →
  这是作者在"拉框架"——用熟悉的经济概念解释陌生的科技概念
  或者——用科技案例说明经济原理
  考题常问："作者为什么提到XX技术？" → 答案往前找，是为了说明某个经济论点
```

## 五、科技经济文章的观点推断词

> 作者不会直接说"I think"——而是用"评价性动词/形容词"暗示态度。

### 正面评价词

| 词 | 用法示意 |
|----|---------|
| **transformative** | *AI's transformative potential across industries is widely recognized.* |
| **breakthrough** | *A breakthrough in battery technology could reshape the energy landscape.* |
| **promising** | *Gene therapy remains a promising but expensive frontier.* |
| **disruptive** | *Disruptive innovation often comes from outside the industry.* |
| **scalable** | *Cloud computing offers scalable solutions for small businesses.* |
| **synergy** | *The synergy between AI and biotech is accelerating drug discovery.* |

### 负面/警惕词

| 词 | 用法示意 |
|----|---------|
| **overhyped** | *Many believe that quantum computing is still overhyped.* |
| **fragile** | *Global supply chains remain fragile despite diversification efforts.* |
| **vulnerable** | *Routine workers are the most vulnerable to automation.* |
| **unsustainable** | *The current trajectory of carbon emissions is unsustainable.* |
| **controversial** | *Gene editing in humans remains highly controversial.* |
| **opaque** | *AI decision-making is often opaque, raising accountability concerns.* |
| **exacerbate** | *Technology can exacerbate existing social inequalities.* |
| **backlash** | *The rapid adoption of AI has sparked a regulatory backlash.* |

### 中立/学术评价词（最高频）

| 词 | 功能 |
|----|------|
| **significant** | 重要的（中性，强调程度） |
| **notable** | 值得注意的 |
| **remarkable** | 显著的 |
| **substantial** | 大量的/实质性的 |
| **modest** | 温和的/适度的（常用于否定乐观预期） |
| **unprecedented** | 前所未有的（中性偏正面） |
| **ambiguous** | 模棱两可的（常指数据/证据不明确） |
| **nuanced** | 需要细致分析的（暗示问题比表面更复杂） |

### 态度词速查表

| 作者态度 | 特征词 |
|---------|--------|
| **科技乐观派** | transformative, breakthrough, promise, potential, unprecedented, game-changer |
| **技术怀疑派** | overhyped, inflated, fragile, vulnerable, exacerbate, uncertain, risky |
| **政策倡导派** | needs regulation, requires oversight, must be addressed, demands action |
| **冷静分析派** | significant, notable, nuanced, ambiguous, mixed evidence, on balance |
| **悲观** | alarming, worrying, dire, catastrophic, unsustainable, irreversible |

## 六、数据与证据引入词

经济科学类文章大量使用数据和研究结果，这些词的后面要么是论据、要么是论证靶子。

### 研究/数据来源

| 词 | 类别 | 例句 |
|----|------|------|
| **according to a study / report** | 研究来源 | *According to a recent McKinsey report, AI could add $13 trillion to global output.* |
| **research indicates / suggests** | 研究发现 | *Research suggests that automation displaces more workers than it creates in the short term.* |
| **the data show / reveal** | 数据显示 | *The data reveal a widening gap between top earners and everyone else.* |
| **evidence points to** | 证据指向 | *Growing evidence points to the negative mental health effects of social media.* |
| **a growing body of research** | 越来越多研究 | *A growing body of research links screen time to reduced attention spans.* |
| **findings** | 研究发现（复数） | *The findings challenge the assumption that technology automatically creates jobs.* |

### 数据解读（重要！）

```
看到"数据/研究结果显示..." →
  策略1：数据本身是什么？（事实问题）
  策略2：作者对数据的解读态度？（观点问题——更重要）
  
  例："GDP grew by 3%" = 事实
     "But this GDP growth masks deepening inequality" = 观点
     
  考点通常在"作者如何看待这个数据"，而不是数据本身。
```

## 七、预测与风险表达

科技经济类文章大量讨论**未来**——预测、趋势、风险、不确定性。

### 预测词

| 确定性 | 词 | 用法 |
|--------|-----|------|
| **高** | is projected / expected / forecast to | *Global EV sales are projected to reach 40 million by 2030.* |
| **中** | is likely to / could potentially | *AI could potentially displace 300 million jobs.* |
| **低** | may / might / could | *Quantum computing may eventually transform cryptography.* |
| **条件** | if... then... / provided that / unless | *Carbon neutrality is achievable, provided that political will exists.* |

### 风险词

| 词 | 用法 |
|----|------|
| **pose a risk / threat to** | *AI poses an existential risk to certain job categories.* |
| **expose vulnerabilities** | *The pandemic exposed vulnerabilities in global supply chains.* |
| **be susceptible to** | *Low-skilled workers are most susceptible to automation.* |
| **a potential pitfall / drawback** | *A potential pitfall of gene editing is off-target effects.* |
| **unintended consequences** | *Algorithmic pricing led to unintended consequences in the housing market.* |
| **trade-off** | *The trade-off between innovation and privacy is at the core of the debate.* |

## 八、科技经济文章六大框架

### 框架 1：技术 → 经济影响（最常见的"横切"式论证）

```
新技术出现 (new technology: AI / gene editing / quantum)
  ↓
技术优势描述 (technical superiority)
  ↓
经济影响分析 (economic impact: productivity, costs, pricing)
  ↓ BUT/However/YET ↓
负面后果/风险 (risks: displacement, inequality, regulation)
  ↓
政策建议 (policy recommendations)
```

> **信号词链：** potential → productivity gains → but → costs / displacement → need regulation

### 框架 2：问题 → 技术方案（政策 × 科技的融合）

```
一个经济/社会问题 (problem: climate change, aging population)
  ↓
为什么传统方案不够 (why traditional solutions fail)
  ↓
技术作为解决方案 (technology as a solution: renewables, AI healthcare)
  ↓ BUT ↓
技术方案的局限 (limitations: cost, scalability, side effects)
  ↓
综合判断 (balanced assessment)
```

> **信号词链：** challenge → traditional approaches → technological solution → however → limitations

### 框架 3：产业颠覆（旧 vs 新）

```
传统产业/模式 (traditional industry: fossil fuels, retail banking, legacy auto)
  ↓
新技术出现 (technology disruption: renewables, fintech, EVs)
  ↓
旧产业的反应 (incumbent response: lobbying, adaptation, resistance)
  ↓
最终格局 (outcome: coexistence / replacement / transformation)
```

> **信号词链：** for decades → but now → disruption → incumbents → response

### 框架 4：科技泡沫与估值争议

```
某项技术被热捧 (technology hype: AI boom, crypto mania)
  ↓
疯狂的投资者行为 (investor behavior: soaring valuations, easy funding)
  ↓
对泡沫的质疑 (skeptical voice: overvaluation, lack of profitability)
  ↓
历史类比 (historical parallel: dot-com bubble, railroad mania)
  ↓
理性分析/判断 (assessment: is this time different?)
```

> **信号词链：** mania / frenzy → valuations → critics warn → parallels to → on balance

### 框架 5：伦理困境

```
技术进步 (technological capability: what technology can do)
  ↓
潜在收益 (potential benefits: efficiency, medical advances)
  ↓ BUT ↓
伦理问题 (ethical concerns: privacy, bias, autonomy, inequality)
  ↓
多方观点 (divergent perspectives: libertarian vs. precautionary)
  ↓
困境本质 (nature of the trade-off: how to balance competing values)
```

> **信号词链：** enabled by → promises → but ethical concerns → opponents argue → moreover

### 框架 6：地缘科技竞争

```
国家的战略目标 (national strategy: economic security, technological leadership)
  ↓
竞争对手的威胁 (rival threat: China catching up, US dominance)
  ↓
政策回应 (policy response: subsidies, export controls, investment)
  ↓
意外后果 (unintended consequences: fragmentation, supply chain disruption)
```

> **信号词链：** strategic priority → in response to → export controls / subsidies → but risks include

## 九、科技经济文章高频语境词组

## 十、实战口诀

```
科技经济阅读，记住三句话：

1. "but 后面才是真的"—— 
   前面夸技术多好，but 后面说问题在哪，这才是考点

2. "例子往前找论点"—— 
   for example 后面是论据，答案在 it 前面的论点

3. "数据是起点不是终点"—— 
   according to data... 后面一定有作者的态度判断（but + 态度词）

补充一条：
4. "跨界对比必有考点"——
   unlike / whereas / by contrast 连接科技和经济两个领域，差异就是出题点
```
