## 一、描述趋势与变化

### 上升（↑）

| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **soar / skyrocket** | 飙升 | *Inflation has soared to a 40-year high.* |
| **surge** | 激增 | *AI investment surged to $2.6 trillion in 2026.* |
| **spike** | 急剧上升 | *Energy prices spiked after the supply disruption.* |
| **rally / rebound** | 反弹/回升 | *Stocks rallied after the Fed paused rate hikes.* |
| **pick up** | 加快/好转 | *Economic growth is expected to pick up in H2.* |
| **gain ground / gain traction** | 获得势头 | *The idea of universal basic income is gaining traction.* |
| **climb / edge up** | 爬升/小幅上升 | *The jobless rate edged up to 3.8%.* |

### 下降（↓）

| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **plunge / plummet** | 骤降 | *The stock plunged 30% in a single day.* |
| **tumble / slump** | 暴跌/不景气 | *Oil prices tumbled as demand fears mounted.* |
| **slide / slip / dip** | 下滑 | *The currency slid to a five-year low.* |
| **shrink / contract** | 收缩 | *The economy contracted for two consecutive quarters.* |
| **take a hit** | 受打击 | *Consumer confidence took a hit from rising prices.* |
| **lose steam** | 失去动力 | *The rally lost steam as earnings disappointed.* |

### 稳定/恢复（→）

| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **stabilize / level off** | 企稳 | *House prices appear to be leveling off.* |
| **bottom out** | 触底 | *The housing market bottomed out in early 2024.* |
| **recover / bounce back** | 复苏/回弹 | *The economy bounced back faster than expected.* |
| **hold steady / flatline** | 保持稳定/持平 | *Wages have flatlined for the bottom half.* |

## 八、写作/翻译高分句型

### 开头引出话题
- *The debate over [X] has intensified in recent years.*
- *Few issues have generated as much controversy as [X].*
- *[X] has emerged as a defining challenge of our time.*

### 表达观点
- *The evidence suggests that...*
- *A growing body of research indicates that...*
- *It is widely acknowledged that...*

### 转折与让步（最经典考研句式）
- ***While [A], [B].*** — 尽管A，但是B
- *For all the benefits of [X], there are costs to weigh.*
- *Yet set against these benefits are a bundle of worries.*

### 因果链条
- *The logic is straightforward: if [A], then [B].*
- *This, in turn, leads to [C].*
- *The net result is [D].*

### 总结建议
- *Policymakers would be well-advised to...*
- *A more nuanced approach is needed — one that balances [A] with [B].*
- *There are no silver bullets; what is needed is a combination of [X] and [Y].*

## 记忆建议

1. 每天一类（8-10个），而不是一次背全部
2. 每学一个表达，用自己关心的经济科技话题仿写一句
3. 写作时有意识替换基础词——surge代替increase，plunge代替decrease，fuel代替cause
4. 比喻表达要理解背后的故事——比如"kick the can down the road"想象踢罐子的画面
