## 4. 长难句逐句拆解（7 句）

### 句 1（P2）

**原句**：As the UK plans to ban under 16s from social media platforms such as Snapchat, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and X, experts writing in The BMJ today argue that restrictions may reduce some harms while shifting others, depending on how they are implemented and enforced.

**结构拆解**：
- 时间/背景状语从句：As the UK plans to ban under 16s from social media platforms ...（"随着/当……"）
- 主干：*experts ... argue that ...*（主谓 + 宾语从句）
- 现在分词定语：writing in The BMJ today（修饰 experts）
- 宾语从句：restrictions may reduce some harms while shifting others（while+现在分词表对比）
- 条件/方式状语：depending on how they are implemented and enforced（depending on + how 从句）

**翻译**：随着英国计划禁止 16 岁以下人群使用 Snapchat、TikTok、Instagram、Facebook 和 X 等社交媒体平台，今天在《英国医学杂志》撰文的专家认为，限制措施可能减少部分危害，同时转移其他危害——具体取决于这些措施如何实施和执行。

**语法点**：as 时间状语 + 分词定语 + 宾语从句 + while+现在分词对比 + depending on how 从句；"reduce some harms while shifting others"是全文核心表达。

**仿写**：*As governments plan to regulate artificial intelligence, experts argue that new rules may solve some problems while creating others, depending on how they are designed and enforced.*（政策评论段开头）

### 句 2（P3）

**原句**：Amrit Kaur Purba and colleagues argue that social media restrictions operate within a wider system of adolescents, families, schools, governments, and commercial actors – and therefore should be treated as complex systems interventions rather than isolated behavioural policies.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*Amrit Kaur Purba and colleagues argue that ...*（主谓 + 宾语从句）
- 宾语从句①：social media restrictions operate within a wider system ...（主谓+介词短语）
- 宾语从句②：and therefore should be treated as complex systems interventions（并列谓语，被动 + treat A as B）
- 对比结构：rather than isolated behavioural policies

**翻译**：Amrit Kaur Purba 及其同事认为，社交媒体限制运行于由青少年、家庭、学校、政府和商业行为者构成的更大系统之中，因此应被视为复杂系统干预，而非孤立的个人行为政策。

**语法点**：宾语从句并列谓语 + treat...as 结构 + rather than 对比；commercial actors 为公卫/社科高频表达。

**仿写**：*Researchers argue that climate policies operate within a wider system of households, firms, governments and international actors – and therefore should be treated as systemic interventions rather than isolated lifestyle campaigns.*（政策分析段）

### 句 3（P4）

**原句**：Without this broader approach, they warn that "governments risk introducing highly visible policies that are poorly understood and may cause unintended harm while leaving root causes unchanged."

**结构拆解**：
- 条件状语（介词短语）：Without this broader approach（"若无这种更整体的方法"）
- 主干：*they warn that ...*（主谓 + 宾语从句）
- 宾语从句：governments risk introducing highly visible policies ...（risk + 动名词）
- 定语从句：that are poorly understood and may cause unintended harm（修饰 policies，并列谓语）
- 对比状语：while leaving root causes unchanged（while + 现在分词）

**翻译**：若不采取这种更整体的方法，他们警告说："政府很可能推出一些高可见度但理解不足的政策，这些政策可能造成意外伤害，同时让根本原因保持不变。"

**语法点**：Without 介词短语表条件 + risk+动名词 + that 定语从句 + while+现在分词对比；unintended harm 为高频搭配。

**仿写**：*Without careful evaluation, policymakers risk introducing highly visible measures that are poorly understood and may cause unintended harm while leaving the underlying problems unchanged.*（政策批判段）

### 句 4（P5）

**原句**：They outline how lessons from other commercial determinants of health such as the tobacco and alcohol industries can help predict how social media companies may adapt politically, scientifically, technologically, and economically after regulation.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*They outline how ...*（主谓 + 宾语从句①）
- 宾语从句①：lessons ... can help predict how ...（how 引导，从句内再嵌套宾语）
- 后置定语：from other commercial determinants of health such as the tobacco and alcohol industries（修饰 lessons，含 such as 举例）
- 宾语从句②：how social media companies may adapt ... after regulation（predict 的宾语）
- 四连副词：politically, scientifically, technologically, and economically

**翻译**：他们阐述了：来自烟草和酒精行业等其他"健康商业决定因素"的经验，如何有助于预测社媒公司在监管之后可能如何在政治、科学、技术和经济层面进行调整。

**语法点**：how 宾语从句嵌套 + 长后置定语 + 四连并列副词（外刊列举式表达）。

**仿写**：*They explain how lessons from previous technological shifts can help predict how industries may adapt socially, economically, and politically after major regulation.*（分析段）

### 句 5（P8）

**原句**：The authors also note that restrictions may not affect all young people in the same way, suggesting that those with supportive families, strong digital skills, access to high quality educational resources, and opportunities for safe offline activities may benefit more than those facing isolation, unsafe environments, or limited support.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*The authors also note that ...*（主谓 + 宾语从句①）
- 宾语从句①：restrictions may not affect all young people in the same way
- 现在分词伴随：suggesting that ...（补充说明"进一步表明"）
- 宾语从句②：those with supportive families, strong digital skills, ... may benefit more than those facing isolation, ...（比较结构，主语带长介词短语并列）
- 后置定语：facing isolation, unsafe environments, or limited support（现在分词修饰第二个 those）

**翻译**：作者还指出，限制措施对年轻人的影响可能并不相同——这表明，拥有支持性家庭、较强数字技能、优质教育资源以及安全线下活动机会的青少年，可能比那些面临孤立、不安全环境或支持有限的青少年获益更多。

**语法点**：宾语从句嵌套 + suggesting that 分词伴随 + 长并列介词短语主语 + more than 比较 + 分词后置定语；这是细节题最爱考的不平等论点。

**仿写**：*The study also notes that remote learning may not benefit all students equally, suggesting that those with stable internet and quiet homes may gain more than those facing crowded or unstable conditions.*（教育公平论述段）

### 句 6（P12）

**原句**：While this approach cannot predict exactly what will happen, it helps show how different parts of a system connect, how these parts may respond to change, and where effects may feedback on one another, they write.

**结构拆解**：
- 让步状语从句：While this approach cannot predict exactly what will happen（While="尽管"）
- 主干：*it helps show ...*（help + 省略 to 的不定式）
- 三个并列宾语从句：how different parts ... connect, how these parts may respond to change, and where effects may feedback on one another（show 的宾语）
- 引述倒装：they write（句末）

**翻译**：他们写道，尽管这种方法无法精确预测将要发生什么，但它有助于展示系统的不同部分如何连接、这些部分如何对变化作出反应，以及效应如何在彼此之间反馈。

**语法点**：While 让步 + what 宾语从句 + 三连 how/where 宾语从句 + 引述倒装；feedback 在此为动词"反馈"。

**仿写**：*While this model cannot predict exactly what will happen, it helps show how different factors interact, how they may respond to policy, and where effects may reinforce one another.*（方法论/分析段）

### 句 7（P13）

**原句**：As such, they recommend that evaluations move beyond standalone measures like screen time or short term changes in mental health to capture wider factors such as school engagement, social connections, industry and platform responses, and longer term effects.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*they recommend that evaluations move beyond standalone measures*（recommend + that 虚拟语气从句，move 为动词原形）
- 举例：like screen time or short term changes in mental health
- 目的状语：to capture wider factors ...（不定式表目的）
- 列举：such as school engagement, social connections, industry and platform responses, and longer term effects

**翻译**：因此，他们建议评估应超越屏幕时间、心理健康短期变化等单一指标，以纳入学业投入、社交连接、行业与平台反应以及长期效应等更广泛的因素。

**语法点**：recommend that + 虚拟语气（宾语从句谓语用原形 move）+ like/such as 双举例 + 不定式目的——虚拟语气是考研语法必考点。

**仿写**：*As such, they recommend that schools move beyond test scores to capture wider factors such as motivation, social skills, and long-term learning habits.*（建议段，注意 that 后动词原形）

## 附录 · 原文（BMJ Group, 2026-07-15）

Effects are not simple and need to be considered within social, technological, commercial, and political contexts to avoid unintended harms.

As the UK plans to ban under 16s from social media platforms such as Snapchat, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and X, experts writing in The BMJ today argue that restrictions may reduce some harms while shifting others, depending on how they are implemented and enforced.

Amrit Kaur Purba and colleagues argue that social media restrictions operate within a wider system of adolescents, families, schools, governments, and commercial actors – and therefore should be treated as complex systems interventions rather than isolated behavioural policies.

Without this broader approach, they warn that "governments risk introducing highly visible policies that are poorly understood and may cause unintended harm while leaving root causes unchanged."

They outline how lessons from other commercial determinants of health such as the tobacco and alcohol industries can help predict how social media companies may adapt politically, scientifically, technologically, and economically after regulation.

For example, companies may try to redefine what counts as "social media" so that it falls outside new regulations, invest more in related or less regulated spaces, and shape policy through lobbying, public messaging, research funding, and marketing.

Adolescents themselves may also adapt by moving to more private or harder to monitor spaces, such as encrypted messaging apps or AI based chat systems.

The authors also note that restrictions may not affect all young people in the same way, suggesting that those with supportive families, strong digital skills, access to high quality educational resources, and opportunities for safe offline activities may benefit more than those facing isolation, unsafe environments, or limited support.

One young person's perspective, who is also an author on the paper, seems to support this view. While acknowledging that social media can be both helpful and harmful, they describe it as "a place where friendships are made, where people can find communities, express themselves, learn new things, and sometimes a place to escape difficult situations."

They add: "I have had friends reach out to me on social media about things they aren't comfortable talking to family members about, and I have done the same. Without social media, what could we have done?"

The authors suggest using systems mapping to anticipate these effects and design more balanced, evidence informed approaches.

While this approach cannot predict exactly what will happen, it helps show how different parts of a system connect, how these parts may respond to change, and where effects may feedback on one another, they write.

As such, they recommend that evaluations move beyond standalone measures like screen time or short term changes in mental health to capture wider factors such as school engagement, social connections, industry and platform responses, and longer term effects.

And they conclude that taking this broader view need not delay action. Instead, "it will help ensure policies are balanced, flexible, evidence informed, and improve over time."
