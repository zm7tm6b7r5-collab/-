## 2.3 段落逻辑梳理

**S1｜引入现象**：特朗普宣布将正式调查欧盟贸易行为，声称欧盟对 Google、Apple 等美企征收数十亿美元罚款不公。
- 段间衔接：开篇即点题（The United States will open a formal investigation…）。
- 论证作用：全文新闻事件核心，先给结论。

**S2｜背景交代（事件）**：欧盟以 Google 违反数字反垄断规则（DMA）为由开出 €8.9 亿（约 10 亿美元）罚单——因其用 Google Play 和搜索引擎把消费者引向自家服务、损害竞争者。
- 段间衔接：因果（The Republican president made the announcement a day after the EU hit Google with a fine…）。
- 论证作用：交代罚款来龙去脉，为双方立场提供事实基础。

**S3｜例证/引证（美方立场）**：特朗普社交媒体长文称美国不是欧洲的"存钱罐"，宣布即刻贸易调查，声称罚款"将被完全逆转"，并预告对欧盟征收"大额关税"。
- 段间衔接：递进（In a lengthy post on social media…）。
- 论证作用：用直接引语呈现美方措辞的对抗性。

**S4｜背景交代（政策工具）**：调查紧接白宫对 60 多个国家加征两位数关税（指其强迫劳动执法不力），依据《1974 年贸易法》第 301 条款。
- 段间衔接：因果/时间（Trump's move comes a day after…）。
- 论证作用：说明"贸易调查"是既有施压工具的一部分，而非孤立动作。

**S5｜反方观点（公司回应）**：Google 发言人 Castañeda 称公司努力遵守 DMA 并对欧委会近期决定表示担忧；Amazon、Apple、Meta、Microsoft 与欧委会均未立即回应。
- 段间衔接：转折（José Castañeda… said…）；"We appreciate the engagement…"。
- 论证作用：呈现企业"既配合又不满"的微妙立场，以及信息缺口。

**S6｜背景交代（欧盟执法史）**：欧盟此次罚款是对 Big Tech 的最新打击，布鲁塞尔在约束科技巨头方面走在世界前列，且不顾特朗普报复风险——关税、格陵兰威胁、动摇北约信任。
- 段间衔接：递进（was the latest major crackdown…；It has done so despite the risk…）。
- 论证作用：把个案放进"欧盟引领全球监管"的宏观叙事。

**S7｜数据支撑（Android 案）**：Google 刚输掉欧盟 €45 亿 Android 反垄断罚款的上诉，该案指控其压制竞争、减少消费者选择。
- 段间衔接：补充（Google had recently lost its appeal…）。
- 论证作用：用既往判例说明美欧罚款冲突的持续性。

**S8｜欧方观点**：欧委会执行副主席 Ribera 称"最好的产品应因更好而胜出"，消费者有权从应用开发者处获知最优报价。
- 段间衔接：直接引用（"The best products should succeed…"）。
- 论证作用：给出欧盟执法的消费者保护逻辑。

**S9｜反方观点（谷歌反驳）**：Google 全球事务总裁 Walker 抨击罚款是"由一小群自私投诉人驱动的产品降级"，并称 DMA 迫使谷歌剥离欧洲用户喜爱的实时搜索功能。
- 段间衔接：转折（assailed the fine as…）。
- 论证作用：呈现企业视角的"监管伤及用户体验"论。

**S10｜概念交代 + 客观收束**：欧盟将 Amazon、Apple、Alphabet、Meta、Microsoft、ByteDance 列为"守门人"；发言人 Regnier 强调公平竞争与消费者选择权；文末以 Alphabet 年收入 4030 亿美元收束。
- 段间衔接：概念定义（The EU describes… as "gatekeepers" that…）。
- 论证作用：通讯社体客观呈现双方说法，不给作者结论，把判断留给读者。

## 2.4 长难句逐句拆解（5 句）

**① 原句**：The Republican president made the announcement a day after the EU hit Google with a fine of 890 million euros, or $1 billion, after it said the technology behemoth broke digital antitrust regulations by setting up Google Play and its ubiquitous search engine to corral consumers toward its own services and apps to the detriment of competitors.
- **结构拆解**：
  - 主干：The Republican president made the announcement。
  - 从句/成分：a day after 引导**时间状语从句**（the EU hit Google with a fine）；其后 after it said 为第二个**时间状语从句**；said 后接**宾语从句**（省略 that）：the technology behemoth broke…；by setting up… 为**方式状语**；to corral… 为**目的状语**；to the detriment of competitors 为**结果/影响状语**。
- **翻译**：这位共和党总统在欧盟对 Google 开出 8.9 亿欧元（约 10 亿美元）罚单的次日宣布了这一消息——此前欧盟称这家科技巨头通过设置 Google Play 和无处不在的搜索引擎，把消费者引向自家服务与应用、损害竞争对手，从而违反了数字反垄断法规。
- **语法点**：双时间状语从句 + 宾语从句省略 that + 方式/目的/结果状语链。
- **仿写**：The company made the announcement a day after the regulator imposed a record fine, after it said the firm violated the rules by steering users toward its own services to the detriment of rivals.（政策事件描述）。

**② 原句**：It has done so despite the risk of incurring the wrath of Trump, who has lashed out at the 27-nation bloc's digital regulations in a broader campaign against Europe: imposing high tariffs, making threats to seize Greenland from Denmark by force, and rattling trust within the NATO military alliance.
- **结构拆解**：
  - 主干：It has done so despite the risk…。
  - 从句/成分：of incurring… 为介词短语作定语；who 引导**非限制性定语从句**修饰 Trump；冒号后 imposing…, making threats…, and rattling… 三个**并列现在分词短语**列举 campaign 内容。
- **翻译**：欧盟这样做，是冒着激怒特朗普的风险的——特朗普在对欧的更广泛施压行动中猛烈抨击这个 27 国集团的数字监管：加征高额关税、威胁以武力夺取丹麦的格陵兰岛、动摇北约军事联盟内部的信任。
- **语法点**：非限制性定语从句 + 冒号引导的并列分词列举。
- **仿写**：The regulator acted despite the risk of provoking powerful companies, who have long attacked its rules in a broader campaign: threatening litigation, lobbying lawmakers, and undermining public trust.（监管话题作文）。

**③ 原句**：The new tariffs are being implemented using Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, which permits the president to impose import taxes and other sanctions against countries found to engage in "unjustifiable," "unreasonable" or "discriminatory" trade practices.
- **结构拆解**：
  - 主干：The new tariffs are being implemented。
  - 从句/成分：using Section 301… 为**现在分词方式状语**；which 引导**非限制性定语从句**修饰 Section 301；found to engage in… 为**过去分词短语**作定语修饰 countries。
- **翻译**：新关税正依据《1974 年贸易法》第 301 条款实施，该条款允许总统对那些被认定从事"不正当""不合理"或"歧视性"贸易行为的国家加征进口税和其他制裁。
- **语法点**：分词方式状语 + 非限制性定语从句 + 过去分词定语。
- **仿写**：The new policy is being enforced under a legal framework, which allows the government to impose penalties on actors found to engage in unfair practices.（政策类作文）。

**④ 原句**：Google's president of global affairs, Kent Walker, assailed the fine as "product degradation driven by a small group of self-serving complainants" that will hurt European businesses and consumers.
- **结构拆解**：
  - 主干：Kent Walker assailed the fine as "product degradation…"。
  - 从句/成分：driven by a small group of… 为**过去分词短语**作定语修饰 degradation；that will hurt… 为**定语从句**修饰 degradation；Kent Walker 为 president 的**同位语**。
- **翻译**：谷歌全球事务总裁肯特·沃克抨击这笔罚款是"由一小群自私的投诉人驱动的产品降级"，并称它将伤害欧洲企业和消费者。
- **语法点**：同位语 + assail…as… + 分词定语 + 定语从句。
- **仿写**：The firm's chief executive assailed the ruling as "innovation delayed by a handful of self-serving rivals," a decision that will hurt consumers.（企业回应类小作文）。

**⑤ 原句**：The EU describes some of the world's leading tech giants — Amazon, Apple, Google parent Alphabet, Meta, Microsoft and TikTok owner ByteDance — as "gatekeepers" that control access for consumers.
- **结构拆解**：
  - 主干：The EU describes … tech giants … as "gatekeepers"。
  - 从句/成分：破折号内 Amazon… ByteDance 为**插入语（同位语列举）**；that control access for consumers 为**定语从句**修饰 gatekeepers。
- **翻译**：欧盟把一些全球领先的科技巨头——亚马逊、苹果、谷歌母公司 Alphabet、Meta、微软和 TikTok 母公司字节跳动——描述为"控制消费者入口的守门人"。
- **语法点**：describe…as… + 破折号插入同位语 + 定语从句。
- **仿写**：The law treats some powerful platforms — Google, Meta, TikTok — as "gatekeepers" that control access for consumers.（定义概念类作文）。

## 2.6 命题角度预测（4 题）

**① 主旨题**：The text mainly reports ___.
- **正确项**：the US decision to investigate EU trade practices over fines on American tech giants, along with both sides' arguments.
- **干扰项设置逻辑**：把"€8.9 亿罚款细节"当主旨（具体事件当主旨）；断言美国关税已经生效（文中是"实施中/预告"，需辨别程度）；断言谷歌承认违规（谷歌明确反驳）。

**② 细节题**：The EU fined Google 890 million euros because the company ___.
- **正确项**：used Google Play and its search engine to steer consumers toward its own services to the disadvantage of rivals（原文 corral… to the detriment of competitors 的同义替换）。
- **干扰项设置逻辑**：refused to pay previous fines（无依据）；abused its dominance in the Android market（这是另一宗 €45 亿旧案，张冠李戴）；failed to cooperate with the US investigation（无中生有）。

**③ 推断题**：It can be inferred from the text that the EU ___.
- **正确项**：continues to lead global efforts to rein in Big Tech despite US pressure（原文 "led the world in reining in… despite the risk of incurring the wrath of Trump"）。
- **干扰项设置逻辑**：will reverse Google's fines（"逆转"是特朗普的单方声称，非事实）；is preparing new fines on ByteDance（未提及）；has weakened its enforcement（与全文相反）。

**④ 词义题**：The word "corral" (in "to corral consumers toward its own services") most probably means ___.
- **正确项**：to steer or channel（引导、聚拢）。
- **干扰项设置逻辑**：to fence in livestock（字面义，语境是消费者而非牲畜）；to block（方向相反）；to punish（无依据）。

## 2.7 今日学习建议

- **重点复习词（8 个）**：levy、corral、behemoth、retaliation、wrath、lash out at、throttle、gatekeeper。复习方式：遮挡释义用原文例句自测；corral 和 gatekeeper 都是"熟词僻义/外刊高频"考点。
- **可直接套用的句型**：
  1. X made the announcement a day after Y…, after it said…（事件时间线 + 原因链，小作文/图表作文通用）。
  2. "The best products should succeed because they're better, not because they're owned by the company running the search engine."（排比对比句，大作文结尾升华）。
  3. It has done so despite the risk of…, who has…（让步 + 定语从句，观点段高级表达）。
- **鼓励反馈**：这篇通讯社稿把"贸易战 + 反垄断"两个热点拧成一股绳，正合考研"去美国化、关注欧盟"的选材趋势；你每天积累的"场景词群"会逐渐变成考场上的条件反射，保持节奏。

**Trump says the US will investigate EU trade practices, claiming the bloc unfairly fined tech giants**

WASHINGTON (AP) — The United States will open a formal investigation into the European Union's trade practices, President Donald Trump said on Friday, claiming the bloc has unfairly levied billions of dollars of fines against Google, Apple and other U.S. tech giants.

The Republican president made the announcement a day after the EU hit Google with a fine of 890 million euros, or $1 billion, after it said the technology behemoth broke digital antitrust regulations by setting up Google Play and its ubiquitous search engine to corral consumers toward its own services and apps to the detriment of competitors.

In a lengthy post on social media, Trump said he has warned the EU about its practice of fining U.S. tech companies. He named Google, Apple, Meta, Amazon and others.

"The United States of America is not a 'PIGGYBANK' for Europe, nor will we allow it to be!" Trump said, adding that his post should serve as notice of an immediate trade investigation "into the practice of 'ROBBING' American Companies and, in turn, the American Taxpayer."

"The European Union will pay a very big price for this illegal and highly unethical conduct, which I have consistently warned them about," he said.

In the post, Trump asserted that the penalties against the companies "will be entirely reversed" and predicted "a substantial TARIFF" would be placed on the EU "at the earliest possible moment."

"Stay tuned!" he said.

**Investigation follows new Trump tariffs**

Trump's move comes a day after the White House announced double-digit tariffs on imports from more than 60 countries, accusing them of inadequately enforcing bans on goods produced by forced labor. The new tariffs replace temporary 10% worldwide import taxes that Trump imposed after the Supreme Court struck down his biggest tariffs.

The new tariffs are being implemented using Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, which permits the president to impose import taxes and other sanctions against countries found to engage in "unjustifiable," "unreasonable" or "discriminatory" trade practices.

José Castañeda, a spokesperson for Google, said the company has worked hard to comply with the EU's Digital Markets Act and has expressed its concerns about the effects of recent decisions by the European Commission.

"We appreciate the engagement by the administration and U.S. government," he said.

Representatives of Amazon, Apple, Meta, and Microsoft did not immediately respond to requests for comment. There also was no immediate comment from the Brussels-based European Commission.

**Trump had threatened retaliation over EU fines**

The EU's billion-dollar fine against Google was the latest major crackdown on Big Tech by Brussels, which has led the world in reining in some of the world's largest companies from Silicon Valley to Beijing.

It has done so despite the risk of incurring the wrath of Trump, who has lashed out at the 27-nation bloc's digital regulations in a broader campaign against Europe: imposing high tariffs, making threats to seize Greenland from Denmark by force, and rattling trust within the NATO military alliance.

Trump had threatened retaliation if American tech companies are penalized.

Google had recently lost its appeal of a $4.5 billion antitrust fine imposed by the EU for throttling competition and reducing consumer choice through the dominance of its mobile Android operating system.

The European Commission, the bloc's executive branch and highest antitrust enforcer, said it was acting in the interest of consumers after an investigation of Google.

"The best products should succeed because they're better, not because they're owned by the company running the search engine. And European consumers have a right to be told by app developers where to sign up to the best offers, even when the app store owner does not get a cut," said Teresa Ribera, the commission's executive vice president for clean, just and competitive transition.

Google's president of global affairs, Kent Walker, assailed the fine as "product degradation driven by a small group of self-serving complainants" that will hurt European businesses and consumers.

He said the EU's Digital Markets Act forces Google "to strip away real-time search features Europeans love — like instant pricing and direct availability for hotels, flights, and restaurants — and dismantle safety protections on Google Play."

The EU describes some of the world's leading tech giants — Amazon, Apple, Google parent Alphabet, Meta, Microsoft and TikTok owner ByteDance — as "gatekeepers" that control access for consumers.

"In the EU, businesses have the right to compete fairly. Gatekeepers have the obligation to ensure a level playing field and consumers the right to choose for cheaper alternative offers," European Commission spokesperson Thomas Regnier said. Alphabet reported $403 billion in revenue last year.
