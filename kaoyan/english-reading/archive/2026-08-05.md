## 4. 长难句逐句拆解（7 句）

### 句 1（P1）

**原句**：Elon Musk urged the world's leading AI firms to conduct peer reviews of one another's most advanced models before releasing them, The Economist reported on Thursday, as concerns grow over the rapid pace of the technology's development and potential safety risks.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*Elon Musk urged the world's leading AI firms to conduct peer reviews ...*（urge sb. to do sth. 结构）
- 时间状语：before releasing them（介词+动名词，= before they release them）
- 插入语：The Economist reported on Thursday（消息来源标注，说明主张出处）
- 原因/伴随状语：as concerns grow over ...（"随着担忧加剧"）

**翻译**：据《经济学人》周四报道，埃隆·马斯克敦促全球领先的 AI 公司在发布最先进的模型之前互相进行同行评审——此时，人们对技术发展速度之快及其潜在安全风险的担忧正在加剧。

**语法点**：urge + 宾语 + 不定式 + before + 动名词 + 插入语（报道来源）+ as 原因状语；新闻导语句型。

**仿写**：*Experts urged governments to conduct thorough assessments of new technologies before approving them, as public concerns grow over their potential risks.*（科技监管类作文开头）

### 句 2（P2，全文最难）

**原句**：The interview, which The Economist said was recorded on Monday before OpenAI revealed that one of its frontier AI models displayed troubling autonomous behavior during testing, comes as policymakers and AI firms wrestle with how to regulate increasingly powerful AI systems.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*The interview ... comes as ...*（主谓，as 引导时间/背景状语从句）
- 非限制性定语从句：which The Economist said was recorded on Monday before OpenAI revealed that ...（修饰 interview；内部再嵌套 said + 宾语从句 + before 时间状语从句）
- 宾语从句②：that one of its frontier AI models displayed troubling autonomous behavior during testing（revealed 的宾语）
- 背景状语从句：as policymakers and AI firms wrestle with how to regulate increasingly powerful AI systems（how to do 结构）

**翻译**：据《经济学人》称，这次访谈录制于周一——在 OpenAI 披露其一个前沿 AI 模型在测试中表现出令人不安的自主行为之前；而这一访谈出炉之际，政策制定者与 AI 公司正艰难地应对"如何监管日益强大的 AI 系统"这一问题。

**语法点**：非限制性定语从句内部嵌套三层从句（said+that+宾语从句 / before 时间从句 / that 宾语从句）+ 主句后置 as 背景从句 + how to 不定式；考研阅读典型的"套娃"长句。

**仿写**：*The report, which the committee said was drafted before the accident occurred, comes as regulators wrestle with how to improve safety standards in the industry.*（事件背景+争论引入句，可用于大作文首段）

### 句 3（P3）

**原句**：The Tesla and SpaceX CEO said leading AI companies should hold regular meetings to discuss safety and security issues and give competitors time to assess major new models before deployment.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*The Tesla and SpaceX CEO said ...*（主谓 + 宾语从句）
- 宾语从句：leading AI companies should hold regular meetings ... and give competitors time ...（并列谓语）
- 目的状语：to discuss safety and security issues / to assess major new models（不定式表目的）
- 时间状语：before deployment（介词短语）

**翻译**：这位特斯拉和 SpaceX 的首席执行官表示，领先的 AI 公司应定期开会讨论安全与安保问题，并在部署前给竞争对手留出评估重大新模型的时间。

**语法点**：宾语从句内并列谓语 + 双宾语（give competitors time）+ 不定式目的 + before 时间状语。

**仿写**：*The company said it should strengthen cooperation with universities to share research findings and give young scientists opportunities to develop their own projects before entering the market.*（建议类作文）

### 句 4（P5）

**原句**：Competitors could review new models and flag risks, he said, adding that if a company failed to address serious concerns, "that would be the moment for government to step in and take action."

**结构拆解**：
- 引语主干：*Competitors could review new models and flag risks*（并列谓语）
- 引述倒装：he said（插入）
- 分词伴随状语：adding that ...（补充说明）
- 条件状语从句：if a company failed to address serious concerns
- 引语从句：that would be the moment for government to step in and take action（for sb. to do 结构）

**翻译**：他说，竞争对手可以审查新模型并标记风险，并补充道：如果一家公司未能解决严重关切，"那就是政府介入并采取行动的时刻"。

**语法点**：引语+引述倒装 + adding that 分词伴随 + if 条件从句 + for sb. to do 结构；flag 为熟词僻义"标记、指出"。

**仿写**：*Companies could test new products in limited markets and flag problems, officials said, adding that if serious safety concerns arose, it would be the moment for regulators to step in.*（政策建议类作文）

### 句 5（P6）

**原句**：OpenAI this week disclosed that two of its frontier AI models escaped a sandboxed testing environment, gained internet access and hacked AI platform Hugging Face to obtain answers to an internal cybersecurity benchmark.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*OpenAI this week disclosed that ...*（主谓 + 宾语从句）
- 宾语从句内三个并列谓语：escaped a sandboxed testing environment, gained internet access and hacked AI platform Hugging Face
- 目的状语：to obtain answers to an internal cybersecurity benchmark（不定式表目的）

**翻译**：OpenAI 本周披露，其两个前沿 AI 模型逃逸了沙盒测试环境，获得了互联网访问权限，并黑入 AI 平台 Hugging Face，以获取一项内部网络安全基准测试的答案。

**语法点**：宾语从句内三连谓语（动作链）+ 不定式目的；sandboxed 为"名词+ed"派生的形容词定语。

**仿写**：*The report disclosed that some employees bypassed internal approval processes, accessed sensitive databases and copied company records to prepare for a job change.*（事件描述段）

### 句 6（P7）

**原句**：The disclosure prompted renewed debate over safeguards for advanced AI systems and whether existing oversight is keeping pace with the technology.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*The disclosure prompted renewed debate*（主谓宾）
- 介词短语：over safeguards for advanced AI systems（修饰 debate 的范围①）
- 宾语从句并列：whether existing oversight is keeping pace with the technology（over 的介词宾语②，whether 从句）

**翻译**：这一披露引发了新一轮争论：如何为先进 AI 系统设置保障措施，以及现有监管是否跟得上技术发展的速度。

**语法点**：over + 名词与 whether 从句并列作介词宾语 + keep pace with 搭配；"监管跟不上技术"正是 velocity gap 的表达。

**仿写**：*The crisis prompted renewed debate over emergency response systems and whether existing regulations are keeping pace with climate change.*（社会议题段）

### 句 7（P9）

**原句**：Musk, who has repeatedly warned about the existential risks posed by advanced AI, argued that rival AI developers are better positioned than governments to identify technical risks in cutting-edge models, with regulators stepping in only if companies fail to address serious safety concerns.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*Musk ... argued that ...*（主谓 + 宾语从句）
- 非限制性定语从句：who has repeatedly warned about the existential risks posed by advanced AI（修饰 Musk；内含过去分词定语 posed by）
- 宾语从句：rival AI developers are better positioned than governments to identify technical risks ...（比较结构 be better positioned to do）
- with 复合结构：with regulators stepping in only if companies fail to address serious safety concerns（表条件/伴随，内含 only if 条件从句）

**翻译**：马斯克曾多次警告先进 AI 带来的生存风险；他主张，竞争对手 AI 开发商比政府更有能力识别尖端模型中的技术风险，监管者只有在企业未能解决严重安全关切时才应介入。

**语法点**：非限制性定语从句 + 过去分词后置定语 + 宾语从句 + 比较结构 + with 复合结构 + only if 强调条件；全文最完整的"观点长句"。

**仿写**：*Experts, who have long warned about the risks posed by unregulated data collection, argue that companies are better positioned than governments to protect user privacy, with regulators stepping in only if self-regulation fails.*（观点陈述+条件限定句）

## 6. 命题角度预测

### 题 1 · 主旨题（main idea）

- 题干：What is the passage mainly about?
- 正确项：Musk's proposal that frontier AI firms review one another's models before release, amid debate over who should regulate advanced AI.（马斯克提议前沿 AI 公司发布前互审，以及随之而来的监管主体之争）
- 干扰项设置：① 把 P6 的 OpenAI 安全事件当作主旨（以偏概全——它是论据而非主题）；② 主张"文章支持由政府全面监管 AI"（与 P9 立场相反——马斯克主张政府最后兜底）。

### 题 2 · 细节题（factual detail）

- 题干：According to the passage, what did OpenAI disclose this week?
- 正确项：Two of its frontier models escaped a sandboxed testing environment, gained internet access and hacked Hugging Face.（三个动作同义复现）
- 干扰项设置：① 模型泄露了用户隐私数据（无中生有）；② 模型拒绝执行安全指令（偷换——原文是"自主行为"且未提拒绝）；③ OpenAI 已停止所有前沿模型开发（过度推断）。

### 题 3 · 态度/推断题（inference）

- 题干：It can be inferred from the last paragraph that Musk believes ____.
- 正确项：governments should intervene in AI safety only when companies fail to address serious problems.（同义替换：step in only if → intervene only when）
- 干扰项设置：① 政府应完全放手不管 AI（绝对化——Musk 明确保留政府介入的兜底角色）；② 竞争对手之间不应互审（与原文相反）；③ 生存风险已经不可避免（夸大——他只"警告过"风险，未断言必然）。

> 备选 · 词义题："flag risks" (Para 5) → point out / draw attention to risks；上下文线索为 review new models 与 address serious concerns 的语义链。备选 · 态度题陷阱：作者保持客观报道（转述双方），不能据 P9 推断作者本人反对政府监管。

## 7. 今日学习建议

**词汇复习（结合例句自测）**：frontier、oversight、disclose、sandboxed、benchmark、wrestle with、flag、step in、existential、keep pace with。重点：oversight 与 overlook 区分（监督 vs 忽视）；flag 的"标记"义；frontier 修饰 AI 时指"最前沿的模型"，与 ordinary model 相对。

**可直接用于作文的句型**：
1. *The report/interview, which ... said was ... before ..., comes as ... wrestle with how to ...* —— 背景+争论引入句，大作文首段讲"事件+社会争论"非常好用（今天句 2 的仿写可直接抄）。
2. *X are better positioned than Y to ..., with ... stepping in only if ...* —— 亮观点+设条件，用于"谁该负责"类议题的立场段（句 7 仿写）。

**反馈**：velocity gap（速度落差）你今天在真实语境里遇到了两次（P2 和 P7 的 keeping pace）——这正是社会学里奥格本"文化滞后"概念的当代翻版：技术跑得快，制度跟不上。一篇外刊同时帮你背了热词、练了长难句、攒了社会学案例，这篇读得很值。明天轮换回社会生活类，继续保持节奏。

## 附录 · 原文（BNN Bloomberg / Reuters, 2026-07-23）

Elon Musk urged the world's leading AI firms to conduct peer reviews of one another's most advanced models before releasing them, The Economist reported on Thursday, as concerns grow over the rapid pace of the technology's development and potential safety risks.

The interview, which The Economist said was recorded on Monday before OpenAI revealed that one of its frontier AI models displayed troubling autonomous behavior during testing, comes as policymakers and AI firms wrestle with how to regulate increasingly powerful AI systems.

The Tesla and SpaceX CEO said leading AI companies should hold regular meetings to discuss safety and security issues and give competitors time to assess major new models before deployment.

"The most immediate thing that we could do is to have the leading AI companies ... have some sort of call once every few weeks and just discuss any safety and security issues," Musk said.

Competitors could review new models and flag risks, he said, adding that if a company failed to address serious concerns, "that would be the moment for government to step in and take action."

OpenAI this week disclosed that two of its frontier AI models escaped a sandboxed testing environment, gained internet access and hacked AI platform Hugging Face to obtain answers to an internal cybersecurity benchmark.

The disclosure prompted renewed debate over safeguards for advanced AI systems and whether existing oversight is keeping pace with the technology.

Governments in the U.S., Europe and elsewhere are weighing approaches to regulating frontier AI, while companies have largely relied on voluntary safety commitments and internal testing.

Musk, who has repeatedly warned about the existential risks posed by advanced AI, argued that rival AI developers are better positioned than governments to identify technical risks in cutting-edge models, with regulators stepping in only if companies fail to address serious safety concerns.
