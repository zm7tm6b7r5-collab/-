## 4. 长难句逐句拆解（7 句）

### 句 1（P2）

**原句**：The legal arguments presented by the plaintiffs echoed some of those brought against big tobacco in the 1990s, which ultimately led to restrictions against tobacco companies targeting ads or products toward young people, among other remedies to restrict their influence.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*The legal arguments ... echoed some of those ...*（主谓宾）
- 后置定语①：presented by the plaintiffs（过去分词修饰 arguments，表被动）
- 后置定语②：brought against big tobacco in the 1990s（过去分词修饰 those，省略 that were）
- 非限制性定语从句：which ultimately led to restrictions ...（修饰前面整件事，指"烟草诉讼带来的结果"）
- 后置定语③：targeting ads or products toward young people（现在分词修饰 restrictions）
- 补充状语：among other remedies to restrict their influence（介词短语 + 不定式表目的）

**翻译**：原告提出的法律论证，呼应了 20 世纪 90 年代针对烟草巨头的那些诉讼；后者最终促成了限制烟草公司向年轻人投放广告和产品等补救措施，以约束其影响力。

**语法点**：多层过去分词后置定语 + which 引导非限制性定语从句指代整句 + 现在分词定语；"echo"在此为熟词僻义"呼应、与……如出一辙"。

**仿写**：*The arguments raised by critics echoed those made against the fast-food industry years ago, which eventually led to stricter labelling rules, among other measures to protect public health.*（作文中做历史类比，增强论证说服力）

### 句 2（P3）

**原句**：The jury ordered the companies to pay $6 million to the plaintiff, a 20-year-old woman identified in court as Kaley G.M.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*The jury ordered the companies to pay $6 million to the plaintiff*（order sb. to do sth. 结构）
- 同位语：a 20-year-old woman（补充说明 plaintiff 身份）
- 后置定语：identified in court as Kaley G.M.（过去分词修饰 woman）

**翻译**：陪审团判令两家公司向原告支付 600 万美元；原告是一名 20 岁女性，法庭上以 Kaley G.M. 代称。

**语法点**：order + 宾语 + 不定式 + 双宾语结构 + 同位语 + 过去分词后置定语（法律新闻高频句式）。

### 句 3（P3）

**原句**：During the trial, Kaley G.M. testified that using social media as a child and as a teenager gave her anxiety and made her feel insecure about her looks.

**结构拆解**：
- 状语：During the trial（时间状语）
- 主干：*Kaley G.M. testified that ...*（主谓 + 宾语从句）
- 宾语从句主语：using social media as a child and as a teenager（动名词短语作主语）
- 并列谓语：gave her anxiety and made her feel insecure about her looks

**翻译**：庭审中，Kaley G.M. 作证说，童年和青少年时期使用社交媒体给她带来焦虑，并让她对自己的外貌缺乏安全感。

**语法点**：动名词短语作主语 + 宾语从句内并列谓语 + make sb. feel 的使役结构。

**仿写**：*In the survey, many students reported that using smartphones late at night disturbed their sleep and made them less focused in class.*（现象描述段，社会调查类作文）

### 句 4（P3）

**原句**：Her lawyers alleged that the features and design of social media apps are intentionally addictive, while "like" buttons feed teens' need for social validation.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*Her lawyers alleged that ...*（主谓 + 宾语从句）
- 宾语从句：the features and design of social media apps are intentionally addictive
- 对比/补充从句：while "like" buttons feed teens' need for social validation（while 表"与此同时/而"，补充说明机制）

**翻译**：她的律师指控称，社交媒体应用的功能与设计是蓄意令人上瘾的，而"点赞"按钮恰恰喂养了青少年对社交认可的需求。

**语法点**：allege 的法律含义（"指控"而非"声称"）+ while 连接对比补充 + feed 的比喻用法（"喂养"需求）。

**仿写**：*Critics argue that recommendation algorithms are deliberately engaging, while short videos constantly feed users' appetite for instant pleasure.*（科技批判类作文，可直接套用）

### 句 5（P4）

**原句**：The case is one of several that are being brought against the social media companies Meta, TikTok, YouTube and Snap on behalf of 1,600 plaintiffs, including hundreds of families and 250 school districts.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*The case is one of several*（主系表）
- 定语从句：that are being brought against the social media companies ...（修饰 several，进行时被动语态）
- 介词短语：on behalf of 1,600 plaintiffs（"代表……"，说明诉讼发起方）
- 现在分词补充：including hundreds of families and 250 school districts（补充 plaintiffs 构成）

**翻译**：该案是代表 1600 名原告（包括数百个家庭和 250 个学区）针对 Meta、TikTok、YouTube 和 Snap 等社交媒体公司提起的众多诉讼之一。

**语法点**：one of + 复数名词 + 定语从句（谓语用复数 are）+ 现在进行时被动语态 + on behalf of 短语——外刊法律报道高频组合。

**仿写**：*The reform is one of several that are being implemented across the country on behalf of rural students, including those from low-income families.*（政策类作文）

### 句 6（P5）

**原句**：Over the course of the seven-week trial, lawyers for Meta and YouTube, which is owned by Google, argued that their platforms are safe for the majority of young users.

**结构拆解**：
- 时间状语：Over the course of the seven-week trial（"在七周庭审期间"）
- 主干：*lawyers for Meta and YouTube ... argued that ...*（主谓 + 宾语从句）
- 非限制性定语从句：which is owned by Google（修饰 YouTube，插入语性质，说明归属）
- 宾语从句：their platforms are safe for the majority of young users

**翻译**：在长达七周的庭审期间，Meta 和 YouTube（后者为 Google 旗下）的律师辩称，他们的平台对绝大多数年轻用户是安全的。

**语法点**：非限制性定语从句作插入语 + 宾语从句 + over the course of 时间短语。

**仿写**：*Throughout the public debate, officials, who are under growing pressure, insisted that the new policy is beneficial to the majority of residents.*（政论类作文）

### 句 7（P7）

**原句**："We disagree with the verdict and plan to appeal. This case misunderstands YouTube, which is a responsibly built streaming platform, not a social media site," said Google spokesperson José Castañeda in a statement.

**结构拆解**：
- 引语部分主干①：*We disagree with the verdict and plan to appeal*（并列谓语）
- 引语部分主干②：*This case misunderstands YouTube*（主谓宾）
- 非限制性定语从句：which is a responsibly built streaming platform（修饰 YouTube）
- 对比否定：not a social media site（对前一定义的补充否定）
- 引述句：said Google spokesperson José Castañeda in a statement（倒装式引述：谓语+主语）

**翻译**："我们不同意这一裁决，并计划上诉。此案误解了 YouTube——它是一个负责任的流媒体平台，而非社交媒体网站，"Google 发言人何塞·卡斯塔涅达在声明中表示。

**语法点**：整段引语作 said 的宾语并前置（倒装引述）+ 非限制性定语从句 + "not ... " 对比否定；responsibly built 为"副词 + 过去分词"复合定语。

**仿写**：*"We firmly disagree with the criticism and will present our evidence. This report overlooks the progress we have made, which is based on years of careful research, not on guesswork," said the project leader in a statement.*（引述反驳观点，可用于立场类作文）

## 附录 · 原文（Scientific American, 2026-03-25）

Meta and YouTube are liable for operating apps that are addictive and damaging to young people's mental health, a jury found in the first-ever trial of its kind to weigh social media's harms.

The legal arguments presented by the plaintiffs echoed some of those brought against big tobacco in the 1990s, which ultimately led to restrictions against tobacco companies targeting ads or products toward young people, among other remedies to restrict their influence.

The jury ordered the companies to pay $6 million to the plaintiff, a 20-year-old woman identified in court as Kaley G.M. Meta was ordered to pay 70 percent of the damages, and YouTube was ordered to pay 30 percent. During the trial, Kaley G.M. testified that using social media as a child and as a teenager gave her anxiety and made her feel insecure about her looks. Her lawyers alleged that the features and design of social media apps are intentionally addictive, while "like" buttons feed teens' need for social validation.

The case is one of several that are being brought against the social media companies Meta, TikTok, YouTube and Snap on behalf of 1,600 plaintiffs, including hundreds of families and 250 school districts. It is a "bellwether trial," meaning its outcome could affect how other lawsuits against social media companies play out.

Before the trial began, TikTok and Snap reached an undisclosed settlement with the plaintiffs involved in the case. Over the course of the seven-week trial, lawyers for Meta and YouTube, which is owned by Google, argued that their platforms are safe for the majority of young users.

"For years, social media companies have profited from targeting children while concealing their addictive and dangerous design features," said the plaintiff's lawyers in a statement released to the media. "Today's verdict is a referendum—from a jury, to an entire industry—that accountability has arrived."

"We disagree with the verdict and plan to appeal. This case misunderstands YouTube, which is a responsibly built streaming platform, not a social media site," said Google spokesperson José Castañeda in a statement.

Meta provided a separate statement to the media in which it said, "We respectfully disagree with the verdict and are evaluating our legal options." The company did not immediately respond to a request for comment from Scientific American.
