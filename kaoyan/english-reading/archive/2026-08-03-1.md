## 4. 长难句逐句拆解（7 句）

### 句 1（P2）

**原句**：Orlando Bravo, the founder of software-focused private equity firm Thoma Bravo, discussed how the role of junior associates is changing as the company increasingly utilizes AI, in a conversation with CNBC's Annette Weisbach at the SuperReturn conference in Berlin on Tuesday.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*Orlando Bravo ... discussed ...*（主语 + 谓语 + how 宾语从句）
- 同位语：the founder of software-focused private equity firm Thoma Bravo（解释主语身份）
- 宾语从句：how the role of junior associates is changing（discussed 的宾语）
- 时间状语从句：as the company increasingly utilizes AI（"随着……"，表伴随变化）
- 背景状语：in a conversation with ... on Tuesday（说明谈话场合，修饰 discussed）

**翻译**：软件私募股权公司 Thoma Bravo 的创始人奥兰多·布拉沃，周二在柏林 SuperReturn 会议上与 CNBC 的安妮特·韦斯巴赫交谈时，讨论了随着公司越来越多地运用 AI，初级助理的职责正在发生怎样的变化。

**语法点**：同位语 + how 引导宾语从句 + as 时间/伴随状语从句，句末叠加长状语（考研阅读人物报道高频句式）。

**仿写**：*In a conversation with journalists at the annual forum, the founder of the tech firm discussed how working habits are changing as artificial intelligence becomes widespread.*（用于作文中引出人物观点，科技类话题开头）

### 句 2（P4）

**原句**："I bother them a lot less, because at midnight I can do something really quickly with AI instead of [comparables], instead of calling them to do it in the middle of the night, which improves their life anyway, which is what they want," he added.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*I bother them a lot less*（主谓宾 + 程度状语）
- 原因状语从句：because ... I can do something really quickly with AI
- 对比结构：instead of [doing comparables], instead of calling them to do it ...（介词短语，表"而不是"）
- 非限制性定语从句①：which improves their life anyway（修饰前面整个事件）
- 非限制性定语从句②：which is what they want（进一步补充说明，内含表语从句 what they want）

**翻译**："我打扰他们的次数少多了，因为半夜里我可以借助 AI 很快完成某些工作，而不是打电话叫他们半夜起来做；这反正改善了他们的生活，而这正是他们想要的。"他补充道。

**语法点**：双重 which 非限制性定语从句（指代整句内容）+ instead of 对比取舍 + 口语化省略；这是考研阅读中典型的"长引语 + 嵌套从句"。

**仿写**：*Remote work bothers employees less, because they can handle urgent tasks at home instead of commuting to the office, which saves time and energy, which is what many young workers want.*（论证远程办公/弹性工作优势）

### 句 3（P5）

**原句**：The comments come amid growing concerns about youth unemployment, with recent data showing the number of young people not in education, employment or training in the U.K. increased to over a million in the first four months of the year.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*The comments come*（主谓）
- 背景状语：amid growing concerns about youth unemployment（"在……日益加剧的背景下"）
- 独立主格（with 复合结构）：with recent data showing ...（with + 名词 + 现在分词）
- 宾语从句（省略 that）：the number of young people ... increased to over a million（showing 的宾语）
- 后置定语：not in education, employment or training（修饰 young people，即 NEET 群体）

**翻译**：此番言论出炉之际，青年失业的担忧正日益加剧——最新数据显示，英国既不读书、也不工作、又不接受培训的年轻人数量，在年内头四个月超过了 100 万。

**语法点**：with + 宾语 + 现在分词（独立主格）表伴随 + 后置定语 + 省略 that 的宾语从句；NEET 完整表达是热点词汇表必背内容。

**仿写**：*The policy comes amid growing concerns about mental health, with recent surveys showing the number of students suffering from anxiety rising sharply over the past two years.*（社会现象类作文引出数据）

### 句 4（P6）

**原句**：Young people are facing an increasingly competitive job market in the U.S. and U.K. as companies lay off thousands of workers and deploy AI, resulting in a shrinking of entry-level jobs.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*Young people are facing an increasingly competitive job market*（主谓宾）
- 原因状语从句：as companies lay off thousands of workers and deploy AI（"由于……"，并列谓语）
- 结果状语：resulting in a shrinking of entry-level jobs（现在分词短语表结果）

**翻译**：由于企业一边裁员数千人、一边部署 AI，美英两国的年轻人正面临竞争日益激烈的就业市场，入门级岗位随之不断萎缩。

**语法点**：as 引导原因状语从句 + 现在分词作结果状语 + 动名词 shrinking 作介词宾语（"动词化名词"是外刊高频现象）。

**仿写**：*Students are facing increasing pressure as universities cut subsidies and raise tuition, resulting in a widening gap in access to higher education.*（教育公平类作文）

### 句 5（P10）

**原句**：While some like Bravo are more bullish on AI's capacity to create jobs, there have been a slew of AI layoffs in the past year, with the technology behind over 50,000 layoffs in the U.S. in 2025.

**结构拆解**：
- 让步状语从句：While some like Bravo are more bullish on AI's capacity to create jobs（"尽管……"）
- 主句：there have been a slew of AI layoffs in the past year（there be 存在句）
- with 复合结构：with the technology behind over 50,000 layoffs（with + 名词短语，点明"技术是背后原因"）

**翻译**：尽管像布拉沃这样的人对 AI 创造就业的能力更为乐观，但过去一年却出现了一连串与 AI 相关的裁员——2025 年美国超过 5 万例裁员背后都有这项技术的身影。

**语法点**：While 引导让步状语从句（"尽管"，对比重心在主句）+ there be 句 + with 名词短语表原因；bullish 为股市用语引申义"看涨/乐观"。

**仿写**：*While some people are optimistic about the benefits of social media, there have been growing cases of online bullying, with teenagers being the most affected group.*（现象利弊分析段，大作文第二段常用）

### 句 6（P11）

**原句**：Meta said in April that it plans to lay off about 10% of its workforce to offset major capex spending on AI infrastructure this year, which the tech giant said could reach up to $135 billion in 2026.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*Meta said ... that it plans to lay off ...*（主谓 + 宾语从句）
- 宾语从句：that it plans to lay off about 10% of its workforce
- 目的状语：to offset major capex spending on AI infrastructure this year（不定式表目的）
- 非限制性定语从句：which ... could reach up to $135 billion in 2026（修饰 capex spending；中间插入 the tech giant said）

**翻译**：Meta 今年 4 月表示，计划裁掉约 10% 的员工，以抵消其在 AI 基础设施上的巨额资本支出——这家科技巨头称，这笔支出 2026 年可能高达 1350 亿美元。

**语法点**：宾语从句嵌套不定式目的状语 + which 引导非限制性定语从句（带插入语 the tech giant said）；capex 是 capital expenditure 的缩写，财经外刊高频。

**仿写**：*Many companies said they would cut costs to offset rising energy prices, which analysts say could continue to climb in the coming years.*（经济类作文数据句）

### 句 7（P16）

**原句**："The truth is this: you're more likely to get a job and get a better-paid job with AI skills, and that is why we're putting such emphasis," she added.

**结构拆解**：
- 主干：*The truth is this*（主系表，冒号引出具体内容）
- 冒号解释部分：you're more likely to get a job and get a better-paid job with AI skills（并列谓语）
- 表语从句：why we're putting such emphasis（由 why 引导，作 is 的表语）

**翻译**："事实是这样的：掌握 AI 技能，你更有可能找到工作，也更有机会获得薪酬更高的工作，这正是我们如此重视（技能培训）的原因。"她补充道。

**语法点**：冒号解释说明 + 并列谓语 + why 引导表语从句；such emphasis 后省略 of upskilling，为口语化省略。

**仿写**：*The truth is this: those who keep learning are more likely to adapt to change, and that is why we should invest in lifelong education.*（论证终身学习/技能转型的结论句）

## 6. 命题角度预测

### 题 1 · 主旨题（main idea）

- 题干：What is the passage mainly about?
- 正确项：Conflicting views on how AI is reshaping entry-level jobs, with a growing emphasis on upskilling.（围绕 AI 对初级岗位的影响呈现正反观点，落点在技能转型）
- 干扰项设置：① 只概括布拉沃的乐观判断（以偏概全，忽略 P5–P6、P10–P12 的担忧与裁员数据）；② 主张"文章认为 AI 将摧毁入门级岗位"（夸大/过度推断——作者保持中立，未下此结论）。

### 题 2 · 细节题（factual detail）

- 题干：According to Orlando Bravo, AI changes the work of junior associates mainly by ____.
- 正确项：freeing them from routine tasks so that they can concentrate on higher-value work.（同义替换：spending a lot less time doing models or comparables → freeing them from routine tasks；get into investing operations → higher-value work）
- 干扰项设置：① 让初级员工工作量成倍增加（无中生有）；② 使初级岗位在市场上普遍大增（偷换——"we need a lot more of them" 仅为布拉沃自己公司的经验，不能推及全市场）；③ 让初级员工直接取代高层管理者（过度推断）。

### 题 3 · 态度题（author's attitude）

- 题干：The author's attitude toward AI's impact on youth employment can best be described as ____.
- 正确项：objective and balanced（客观中立：乐观派引语、裁员数据、政府对策三线并置，未明显站队）
- 干扰项设置：① strongly optimistic（只看前半段，忽略 P10–P12 裁员数据）；② deeply pessimistic（夸大担忧部分，忽略政府对策与技能溢价数据）；③ indifferent（全文对议题有明显呈现与组织，绝非漠不关心）。

> 备选 · 词义题："bullish" (Para 10) 最可能指 optimistic about...；上下文线索为 While 让步对比（乐观 vs 裁员现实）+ "capacity to create jobs" 的正面语义场。备选 · 推断题陷阱：由"AI 技能薪酬高 25%"不能推出"凡掌握 AI 技能者必然高薪"（绝对化推断）。

## 7. 今日学习建议

**词汇复习（结合例句自测，遮释义看例句说出词义）**：bullish（看涨/乐观）、grunt work（杂活）、offset（抵消）、upskill（技能升级）、command（获得——僻义）、lay off（裁员）、mature（成熟——一词多义）、a slew of（大量）。建议把 bullish 与热点清单的 middle-skill collapse、job polarization 放进同一个"就业场景"词群记忆。

**可直接用于作文的句型**：
1. *While some people are optimistic about X, there have been growing concerns about Y, with ... doing ...* —— 让步+转折+数据，用于大作文第二段"利弊分析"，一网打尽两方观点。
2. *The truth is this: ... , and that is why ...* —— 用于结尾段强调因果、亮明立场（今天的句 7 仿写可直接抄入素材本）。

**反馈**：今天这篇与热点清单第 1 号热点完全对应，NEET、upskill 这些词直接在真实语境里复现了；而且从社会学视角看，"AI 削平职业阶梯"正是劳动社会学"职业结构变迁"的当代案例——一篇外刊同时喂了英语和专业课。明天轮换到社会生活类，继续保持节奏。

## 附录 · 原文（CNBC, 2026-06-10）

Thoma Bravo's billionaire founder says AI will transform junior workers' roles by eliminating grunt work, despite growing concerns about the technology's impact on entry-level roles.

Orlando Bravo, the founder of software-focused private equity firm Thoma Bravo, discussed how the role of junior associates is changing as the company increasingly utilizes AI, in a conversation with CNBC's Annette Weisbach at the SuperReturn conference in Berlin on Tuesday.

The jobs of junior associates will become greater, and they'll "mature a lot quicker," Bravo said. "They're spending a lot less time doing models or comparables than before...overall, now they really get into investing operations and a much bigger way of thinking about business."

"I bother them a lot less, because at midnight I can do something really quickly with AI instead of [comparables], instead of calling them to do it in the middle of the night, which improves their life anyway, which is what they want," he added.

The comments come amid growing concerns about youth unemployment, with recent data showing the number of young people not in education, employment or training in the U.K. increased to over a million in the first four months of the year.

Young people are facing an increasingly competitive job market in the U.S. and U.K. as companies lay off thousands of workers and deploy AI, resulting in a shrinking of entry-level jobs.

"For young people, AI is going to be amazing, and I'm very, very upset that some people say that it'll destroy entry-level jobs," Bravo said.

"If you define the role of an associate as just doing a spreadsheet, you don't need that, but our associates are now calling on companies a lot more. They're developing relationships with CEOs, and we need a lot more of them."

Bravo explained that it's the first time in his 30-year career in private equity where he has needed to hire more, as AI creates more work.

While some like Bravo are more bullish on AI's capacity to create jobs, there have been a slew of AI layoffs in the past year, with the technology behind over 50,000 layoffs in the U.S. in 2025. Major firms like Salesforce, IBM, and Microsoft cited AI as a reason for job cuts.

Meta said in April that it plans to lay off about 10% of its workforce to offset major capex spending on AI infrastructure this year, which the tech giant said could reach up to $135 billion in 2026.

Meanwhile, Jack Dorsey's Block laid off over 4,000 people, or over half of its workforce, saying it can operate more efficiently with a smaller team as AI automates more work.

As young people struggle to secure jobs, the U.K.'s technology secretary Liz Kendall said the government is focused on upskilling young workers on AI by providing free courses to help boost employment.

Entry-level workers with AI skills can command salaries up to 25% higher, according to the most recent data from the world's largest recruitment firm, Randstad.

"We will help people through the jobs transition, we will give people the skills, we'll redesign those entry-level jobs," Kendall said in a conversation with CNBC's Ritika Gupta on "Squawk Box Europe."

"We've got a goal of upskilling 10 million workers by 2030. That is a third of the workforce, and already we've delivered 1.7 million AI skills courses. The truth is this: you're more likely to get a job and get a better-paid job with AI skills, and that is why we're putting such emphasis," she added.
